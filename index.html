01 / REFERENCE STATUS

ABOUT THIS REFERENCE

This page describes the present behavior of an independent 308 Construction website design preview, not the official company website. It is a reference draft, not an approved or legally operative privacy policy of 308 Construction.

Business privacy practices, legal responsibility and processing arrangements cannot be inferred from a web design. They require confirmation by the company before public adoption.

02 / CONTACT INQUIRIES

CONTACT FORM

The demo Contact form asks for a name, email address and message. Phone and subject are optional. Input is validated in the visitor's browser.

Current behavior: with no connected receiving endpoint, submitting valid fields opens a prepared email draft addressed to Main@308gc.com. The visitor must send the message through their own mail application. The demo does not store, transmit or confirm receipt of a contact-form submission.

Email providers and recipients handle sent messages under their own systems; those practices are not established by this preview.

03 / EMPLOYMENT APPLICATIONS

CAREERS & FILES

The Careers application asks for applicant contact details, position selection, a résumé and a cover letter. It also offers optional availability, city/state, professional profile and experience fields. PDF, DOC and DOCX uploads are checked locally for format and size.

Current behavior: its application receiver is not configured, so selected documents are not submitted to this demo. The preview does not request identification documents such as passports, photo ID or Social Security numbers.

Secure transmission, storage, retention and the final application process must be approved before accepting actual candidatures.

04 / HOSTING & RESOURCES

EXTERNAL SERVICES

This preview is served through GitHub Pages. Pages request assets from third parties, including fonts, scripts, photographs and the published company logo. Following map, email, telephone or external links may also involve other providers.

Those providers may receive technical request information under their own terms. Cookies, traffic analytics and third-party tracking have not been fully audited for this demo. No unsupported claim is made about the official company's data practices.

The on-page assistant currently returns predefined answers rather than submitting questions to a live customer-support service.

05 / REQUIRED BEFORE APPROVAL

PENDING CONFIRMATION

Before an official privacy notice can be published, an authorized company representative must verify:

  • Responsible legal entity and privacy contact.
  • Purpose and lawful basis for inquiry and recruitment data.
  • Recipients, processors, hosting and international transfers.
  • Retention, deletion and applicant-document security.
  • Cookies, analytics and visitor choices.
  • Applicable privacy rights and request procedures.
  • Security testing, live form endpoints and final legal approval.

No official policy is being asserted by listing these unresolved items.

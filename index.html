Project Case Study 01

University of North Texas

Matthews Hall Renovation

LOCATION Denton, TX
SCOPE Approx. 4,000 sq. ft.
KEY FEATURES Offices, ADA Restrooms, Lighting & Finishes

Project Overview

308 Construction renovated a former child development center at UNT Matthews Hall into the College of Education dean’s suite. The multi-trade project covered approximately 4,000 square feet.

Published work included abatement, wall demolition, new offices, a breakroom, ADA restrooms, LED lighting, LVT flooring and feature wall treatments.

Project Details

Client: University of North Texas
Category: Higher Education
Contractor: 308 Construction
Project Case Study 02

Texas Woman's University

Old Main Skylight & Plaster Repair

LOCATION Denton, TX
SPECIALTY Plaster Repairs and Skylight Glass
KEY FEATURES Skylight Detailing, Plaster, Paint Work

Project Overview

308 Construction worked on plaster repairs in Texas Woman’s University Old Main. The project included evening abatement of areas with falling plaster followed by repair and painting the next day.

The published scope describes three-story scaffolding, multiple shifts, extensive coordination with occupants and replacement of skylight glass in the rotunda.

Project Details

Client: Texas Woman's University
Category: University Building Repairs
Contractor: 308 Construction
Project Case Study 03

Plano ISD

Bleacher Upgrades & Facility Renovations

LOCATION Plano, TX
CATEGORY K-12 Athletic Facilities
KEY FEATURES Electrical, Paving and Bleachers

Project Overview

308 Construction provided upgrades to the softball and baseball fields at Plano Senior High School, Plano East High School and Plano West High School.

Across six job sites, the work included electrical, demolition, new concrete paving and new or retrofitted bleachers.

Project Details

Client: Plano ISD
Category: K-12 Athletic
Contractor: 308 Construction
